# Studio Empire — Foundational Core

**Status:** Locked product law  
**Audience:** Design + engineering  
**Rule:** Everything in this game is **native core content**.

---

## Foundational instruction

Everything described for Studio Empire is part of the **core game**.

These systems are **not**:

- Mods
- Add-ons
- Optional expansion content
- External dependencies
- Features installed after release
- Separate game modes

**Platform history, research, engine development, employee training, custom hardware, pricing, industry events, offices, R&D, AAA development, MMOs, subscriptions, and future technology** are all native parts of the complete game.

Reviewed Game Dev Tycoon / Expansion Pack material is **research only** — used to understand possible systems, dates, progression, and balancing. Studio Empire implements improved versions of those ideas **inside its own architecture**.

---

## One continuous progression

The player experiences a single continuous arc:

1. Garage developer  
2. Small studio  
3. Expanded studio  
4. Technology park  
5. R&D and hardware departments  
6. AAA production  
7. Proprietary platforms  
8. Online ecosystems  
9. MMOs and live services  
10. Subscriptions and publishing  
11. Future technology  
12. Endless sandbox  

Nothing should appear or be described to the player as an “expansion-pack feature.”

---

## Engineering rules

1. **No player-facing expansion labels** — UI, news, tooltips, and tutorials never say “DLC,” “expansion pack,” “mod required,” or “install content.”
2. **Progressive unlock ≠ separate product** — late systems are gated by year, office tier, research, capital, and mastery, not by optional packs.
3. **Internal metadata only** — any historical `source: "expansion"` / pack tags in content files are research provenance for authors; they must not drive storefront framing or player messaging.
4. **One architecture** — platforms, research, engines, training, hardware, live services, and futures share the same save, market, and lifecycle systems.
5. **GDT is not the product** — reimplement the fantasy; do not port pack structure.
