- `START_YEAR` moved from **1982 → 1979**.
- Platform catalog years corrected to historical generations (fictional names kept).
- New early platforms: **Itara** (1977), **IntelliVision** (1979), **Odyssey²** (1978).
- Garage opens with **PC + Itara** (TES no longer day-one; arrives 1985).
- Engine components gain `minYear` gates (e.g. 3D V1 from 1993, Online Play from 1999).
- Research enforces `minYear` in `startResearch`.
- Office pacing floor shifted to 1981 M10.
- Save key/version → v6 (old 1982 saves do not silently migrate).
